  • Energy Advice → Bills, meters and tariffs, and keeping a rented home warm without paying more than you need to.

About the Advice Service

Whether you are an undergraduate or postgraduate, from the UK or overseas, straight out of school or college or returning to study, we provide advice tailored to your needs and help guide you through the issues you are facing.

The service is free, confidential, non-judgemental, independent and non-discriminatory. No information will be shared with the University of Reading or anyone else without your permission.

We are members of AdviceUK, the National Association of Student Money Advisers and the National Homelessness Advice Service, and have held the Advice Quality Standard since 2013.

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 999.
