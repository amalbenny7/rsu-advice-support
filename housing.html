Housing

Finding somewhere to live, signing a contract, getting repairs done and leaving at the end of the year. Our Housing Advisers explain what your landlord has to do, what you have to do, and what your options are when something goes wrong.

The service is free, confidential and independent of the University, so we can discuss your case with you in private.

Get advice →

What we can help with

  • Searching for accommodation → Where to look, what to budget for, and how to avoid the scams that target students looking for a house share.
  • Private rental → Step by step from viewing a property to moving in, plus deposits, guarantors, Council Tax and private halls.
  • Disrepair → What your landlord must repair, damp and mould, how to report a problem, and the four steps to take if the repairs are not done.
  • Leaving your tenancy → Giving notice, leaving before you move in, finding a replacement tenant, and moving out with your deposit intact.
  • Renters’ Rights Act → What the new legislation changed about your contract, your rent, pets and evictions – and what stayed the same.

Talk to an adviser

If you would like further advice or to speak with somebody in person then a Students’ Union Adviser may be able to help. The specialist advisers offer a confidential service, independent from the University and can discuss your case with you in confidence.
